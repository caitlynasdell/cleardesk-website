Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you work regular 9–5 business hours?

ClearDesk works on a flexible remote schedule, including some evenings and weekends. Work is completed according to agreed deadlines, and messages are acknowledged within one business day, Monday through Friday.

Do you provide live phone answering or emergency support?

No. ClearDesk specializes in asynchronous administrative tasks, organization, trackers, and workflow support—not continuous phone coverage or urgent response.

Are weekly packages unlimited support?

No. Each package covers an agreed set of tasks and deliverables. If your needs change, we can discuss adjusting the scope and price together.

Are the portfolio examples from actual clients?

No. They are demonstration projects using fictional information to show the kinds of systems ClearDesk can build.
